## 🧠 About Me

I'm a **Generative AI Engineer and CS PhD Researcher at UNIFESP**, where I was invited to enter directly into the PhD program following my interdisciplinary undergraduate cycle. My research and engineering work focus on **efficient LLM post-training**, **multi-agent systems**, and **real-world AI operationalization** — bridging scientific rigor with production delivery.

Along the way, I've conducted FAPESP-funded research in partnership with **Serasa Experian** and **Siemens Energy**, worked as a Visiting International Research Student at **Dalhousie University** (Canada), and founded/managed the Data & AI department at **SciTec Jr.**

---

## 💼 Experience & Impact

- **Natural Language Processing Researcher** — *PARANA Research Group | FAPESP | Serasa Experian* `(Nov 2024 – Present)`
  - Increased correlation with human judgment by **113%** in reference-free financial news summarization evaluation via LLM task decomposition in **Python** and **LangChain**.
  - Accelerated multi-task retraining cycles by **99.5%** (from `1h17min` to `25s`) while preserving **94.7%** of Macro-F1 through SLM **Model Merging** via **Mergekit**.
  - Boosted relative Macro-F1 by **31.2%** in imbalanced multi-class toxicity detection by structuring synthetic data augmentation pipelines with LLMs.
  - Architected fine-tuning (**Full, LoRA, IA3, Soft Prompts** via **Hugging Face**) and inference (**vLLM**) pipelines across multi-GPU NVIDIA nodes in an HPC Linux environment via **Slurm**.

- **Data and AI Department Manager** — *SciTec Jr.* `(Dec 2024 – Jan 2026)`
  - Founded and structured the Data Science & AI department, leading its spin-off from the computing division.
  - Managed the technical delivery of **6 projects** (3 external consulting engagements and 3 internal automation products) and upskilled consultants in **Python, Pandas, CRISP-DM, Streamlit, and LangChain**.

- **Generative AI Engineer** — *Ativamente (Enactus UNIFESP)* `(Feb 2025 – Jul 2025)`
  - Cut clinical report generation time by **over 90%** (from `1–3h` to `5 min`) by building a **Streamlit** interface and automated LLM summarization pipeline.
  - Built an intelligent **Text-to-SQL** conversational agent for on-demand natural language queries over cognitive workshop data and medical records.

- **Prescriptive Maintenance & XAI Researcher** — *PARANA Research Group | FAPESP | Siemens Energy* `(Jul 2023 – Dec 2024)`
  - Modeled industrial sensor time-series degradation signals using **Linear Regression, XGBoost, and LSTM** for proactive failure prevention.
  - Implemented an **Explainable AI (XAI)** framework with **SHAP, ALE, and PDP**, and co-created the [IAsmin](https://blog-ia-unifesp.vercel.app/) educational platform.
